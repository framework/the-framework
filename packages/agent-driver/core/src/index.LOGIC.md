The package's public entry point: everything a caller (the runner in `packages/agent-runner`, the scheduler in `packages/agent-scheduler`, the product in `packages/framework`) or an adapter (`packages/agent-driver/claude`, `packages/agent-driver/codex`, `packages/agent-driver/github`) may import. That is the contract's vocabulary from `types.ts` with its transient-reason rule and the names of the three parts of the person's own setup every adapter takes; the fake driver; the question and its continuation prompt; the inbox with the end of a turn that drains it; the session log (with its card and diary file names, its diary line, the attaching of a log to a driver session, and `DIARY_ENV` (`AGENT_DIARY`) with the rule that puts the diary's path in the agent's environment); the readiness check from `ready.ts` (is a coding agent's CLI installed and logged in, asked with the questions an adapter hands in) with the real CLI probe it uses; and the pieces an adapter builds its driver with: the progress-event reporter, the folding of framing and stop requests, reading a file back from the directory, and the process core of `cli-session.ts` with its parser and spawn seams. No coding agent's driver is exported here: each is in its adapter's package.
