What the tests cover, with every time made in the machine's own local time, so they hold in any time zone:

- **An interval** - `15m`, `6h`, `2d`, `2w` (14 days) and `1mo` (30 days) each read with their count, unit and length; a leading zero is dropped from the text (`06h` reads as `6h`); `9999mo` reads; `0h`, `0mo`, a unit alone, a number alone, a space inside, `1y`, a negative count, a fraction, a plain word, empty text, `10000d` and two far larger counts are no interval.
- **A time of day** - `10:00`, `9:05` (kept as `09:05`) and `23:59` read; `24:00`, `10:60`, `10`, `10:0`, `10am` and empty text are none; a time of day may go with `2d`, `2w` and `1mo`, not with `15m` or `6h`.
- **The pace in force** - with no pace pick it is the skill's interval, and none for a command with a check and no interval; a pace pick of `30m` is that interval; a pace pick of `2d` at `10:00` is that interval with its time of day and the time it counts from; a time of day beside `6h` is not followed, the interval alone paces; "whenever there is work" gives no pace for a command with a check, and leaves the skill's interval for a command with none; a saved value edited by hand into something that cannot be read (an interval that is a word, a number or a count far above 9999, a "whenever there is work" that is a word, plain text, a number, null, a list) leaves the skill's interval; a "whenever there is work" that is false beside an interval of `2d` is read by its interval.
- **Due from, without a time of day** - a command never started is due at once; one last started at 09:00 with a pace of 6 hours is due from 15:00; a pace of 2 days counts hours like any interval (last started on the 8th at 09:30, due from the 10th at 09:30).
- **Due from, with a time of day** - with "every 2 days at 10:00", a start on the 8th at 10:02 and one on the 8th at 23:50 are both due from the 10th at 10:00; a week adds 7 days (the 8th, then the 15th) and a month 30 (the 1st of October, then the 31st; the 31st of January, then the 2nd of March); a daily pace last started on the 31st of December at 23:50 is due from the 1st of January at 10:00; a last start in the future, the 9th seen from the 8th, is counted from like any other (the 11th at 10:00).
- **A missed time** - with "every day at 10:00", last started on the 5th, a machine that wakes on the 8th at 08:00 finds the command due (from the 6th at 10:00, already past); having started then, the command is next due on the 9th at 10:00, not on the 8th at 10:00 as well.
- **Nothing before the first time of day** - "every day at 10:00" picked at 11:00 on the 8th, for a command never started, is due from the 9th at 10:00; picked at 09:00, or at 10:00 sharp, from the 8th at 10:00; picked half a minute past 10:00, from the 9th at 10:00; a time moved from 10:00 to 16:00 at 11:00, after that day's start at 10:00, is due from the 9th at 16:00, with no second start that day; picked at 11:00 for a command last started a week before, still from the 9th at 10:00, not at once; with no record of when it was picked and never started, from today's 10:00.
- **Counting from the switch** - a pace pick of "every day at 10:00" made on the 5th and counted from the 7th at 15:00 keeps its interval and its time and counts from then: a command never started is then due from the 8th at 10:00, where the pick as it was would be due from the 6th at 10:00, long past; a pace pick with no time of day, "whenever there is work", none, null and plain text are answered unchanged.
- **As a decision writes them** - a pace of 6 hours is `6h`, a pace of 2 days at 9:05 is `2d at 09:05`, and a local time is `2026-10-10 09:05`.
