What the tests cover, on readings alone:

- **The reset prose** - `Jul 25 at 7am (UTC)`, minutes, `12am` and `12pm`, the comma phrasing newer Claude Code prints, a named zone in summer and across a daylight-saving change, and the year picked nearest now across the new year; prose that cannot be placed (`soon`, a month that is not one, `13am`, an unknown zone) reads as unknown.
- **The boundary** - the percentage rises continuously with the elapsed share of the week (0 at the first instant, 25 at 1.75 days, 100 only at the exact reset, six sevenths a day before), the day steps at the week's own hour and a week already over reads as day 7.
- **The windows in force** - the account's week is measured against the boundary; the selected model's own week binds too and only that model's, and no model means only the account's week.
- **No reading** - no windows, a week with no reset prose, and prose that cannot be placed all answer no status.
- **The limit** - it is the boundary until moved; a cushion moves the line work stops at without moving the boundary, pulled back below what is spent stops the work and pushed forward frees it; a limit dragged past either end of the week stops at 0 or 100.
