What the tests cover:

- **A link home** - the brand is a link whose address is `/`.
- **A plain click stays in the app** - a plain click navigates through the shell's router and the browser's own navigation is suppressed.
- **A modified click is the browser's** - a click with Cmd, Ctrl, Shift or Alt, or a middle click, is not suppressed and does not navigate in-app, so the browser can open a second Overview.
- **The wordmark folds away** - the wordmark keeps its hidden-below-small-width styling while the mark itself stays visible.
- **The working state** - the mark is drawn in its working (animated) form while an agent is working and in its plain form otherwise.
