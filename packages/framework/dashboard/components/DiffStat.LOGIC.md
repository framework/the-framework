The size of a change as the "+added −removed" pair, the one way the dashboard says it: in an agent's handoff summary and its list of changed files, and, through `framework/module`, wherever a module shows a change (the Files module's hover card and its count of an agent's changed files). "+<added>" is green and "−<removed>" red, in monospace with aligned digits; a count of zero is omitted, and a space separates the two only when both show.
