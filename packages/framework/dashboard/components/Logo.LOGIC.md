The Framework's mark, the six-strand hexknot from the brand site, drawn as the same shape in two states: idle, and working while an agent [1] is running. The mark never moves; only its color says whether the AI is at work, and its label says so in words.

## Glossary

[1] agent: the unit of work: one task worked by a coding agent under The Framework's control — in its own checkout, on its own branch, streaming events, handed off when it ends.

## Business logic — TL;DR

- **Idle: the neutral ramp** - each strand is a flat fill from a neutral ramp, taken from theme variables so that on a dark background the ramp is lightened and no strand sinks into the canvas; the strands overlap at the knot's crossings, which is why they are opaque fills rather than one color at varying opacity.
- **Working: the brand hues sweep the knot** - each strand is painted from its own gradient whose two color stops sit one brand hue apart and cycle through the six brand hues over 6 seconds, every cycle closing on the hue it opened with so the loop never jumps; the paths are the same as idle, so nothing moves.
- **The label says which state it is** - hovering the mark reads "AI is working for you 🚀" or "AI isn't working for you 💤"; its spoken name for assistive technology is the same sentence without the emoji.
