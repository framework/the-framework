What the tests cover, for The Framework's mark:

- **Idle** - the idle mark paints its six strands from the neutral theme ramp and animates nothing.
- **Working** - the working mark paints every strand from its own animated gradient: six gradients, two color stops each, one animation per stop.
- **A seamless loop** - every hue cycle runs through all six brand hues and closes on the hue it opened with, so the loop never jumps.
- **The label** - the label reads "AI is working for you 🚀" when working and "AI isn't working for you 💤" when idle, and the mark's spoken name is the working label without the emoji.
