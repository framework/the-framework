What the tests cover:

- **Before a reading** - the panel says "Reading your usage" and draws no track, rather than an empty week that would read as "nothing used".
- **The week track** - the track's accessible label names the spent share, the boundary percentage and the day of seven; one two-letter label per calendar day the week touches, with a mid-day-start weekday labeled once at the end where most of it falls ("WE" through "TU"), and a notch between consecutive days; exactly one track, and it is the account's week; the 5-hour session window is reachable only through "show all limits".
- **Used and projected segments** - the solid fill is the spent share, the dimmed segment starts where the fill ends and spans up to the limit (boundary plus offset), neither rounds its own corners, and no dimmed segment is drawn once the limit is at or below what was spent.
- **The handle** - it is one slider drawn on the track itself, labeled "Unattended work stops at", valued on the track's 0–100 scale so it rests exactly on the boundary at offset 0; a move stores the distance from the boundary as the spend offset; successive moves accumulate and the drawn limit follows the handle without waiting for the next reading; a drag past the track's far end stores +50, past the near end −50; no handle when there is no track.
- **Unreadable quota** - when Claude Code is not found the panel says so and shows neither a track nor a handle; a week reported without a reset time says "no reset time" and names that week rather than denying it exists; a readout with no week at all says "Couldn't parse quota", names the missing "Current week (all models)" line and quotes the windows that did arrive; a reset phrasing the daemon cannot place is a red alert quoting the phrasing, with the reported windows still listed and no handle; an unrecognized readout keeps the earlier track and says its numbers are "from the reading before it" and when it was "Last read"; an unrecognized readout with nothing retained says it is "Trying again shortly".
- **The figures** - the pace deviation reads as a duration ("Under-consuming: 2d"), never as a percentage used; the duration is bold and colored like the track while the label is plain; exactly on pace reads "Over-consuming: 0s" and ahead of pace "Over-consuming: <n>d"; half the week used reads "3d spent"; 60% used on day four reads "105% of pace", colored like the track; no pace share at the very start of the week; the deviation's tooltip says "You are 2 days below the quota boundary" and that the user is under-consuming; the reset reads as a weekday and time, with no underline on its trigger.
- **Legend and status** - the legend names "Budget for Autonomous AI" and "Quota boundary" with a help icon; the boundary tooltip explains that matching it means spending exactly at the week's pace and ends on the fun fact about its position; with room left the status reads "enabled" with "move slider to the left to disable", with none "disabled" with "move slider to the right to enable", each with its own explanatory tooltip; the legend and the status share one row.
- **Eager consumption** - no warning at offset 0 nor for a lead smaller than a day; "⚠️ Eager consumption" appears once the limit is a full day ahead, its tooltip naming the real lead ("1 day" at 20 points, "2 days" at 40), placed beside the status and before it.
- **"show all limits"** - every window is listed in the order reported, as a real table with two columns per row; the item is absent when only the week was reported.
- **No roadmap toggle** - the panel carries no "Spend what's left on the roadmap" toggle.
