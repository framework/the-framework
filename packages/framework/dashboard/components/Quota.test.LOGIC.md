What the tests cover:

- **Before a reading** - the panel says "Reading your usage" and draws no track, rather than an empty week that would read as "nothing used".
- **The week track** - the track's accessible label names the spent share, the boundary percentage and the day of seven; one two-letter label per calendar day the week touches, with a mid-day-start weekday labeled once at the end where most of it falls ("WE" through "TU"), and a notch between consecutive days, beside the used fill and the boundary line; exactly one track, and it is the account's week; the 5-hour session window is reachable only through "show all limits".
- **Unreadable quota** - when Claude Code is not found the panel says so and shows no track, still listing the windows reported; a week reported without a reset time says "no reset time" and names that week rather than denying it exists; a readout with no week at all says "Couldn't parse quota", names the missing "Current week (all models)" line and quotes the windows that did arrive; a reset phrasing the daemon cannot place is a red alert quoting the phrasing, with the reported windows still listed; an unrecognized readout keeps the earlier track and says its numbers are "from the reading before it" and when it was "Last read"; an unrecognized readout with nothing retained says it is "Trying again shortly".
- **The figures** - the pace deviation reads as a duration ("Under-consuming: 2d"), never as a percentage used; the duration is bold and colored like the track while the label is plain; exactly on pace reads "Over-consuming: 0s" and ahead of pace "Over-consuming: <n>d"; half the week used reads "3d spent"; 60% used on day four reads "105% of pace", colored like the track; no pace share at the very start of the week; the deviation's tooltip says "You are 2 days below the quota boundary" and that the user is under-consuming; the reset reads as a weekday and time, with no underline on its trigger.
- **The legend** - the legend names "Used" and "Quota boundary" with a help icon; the boundary tooltip explains that matching it means spending exactly at the week's pace and ends on the fun fact about its position.
- **"show all limits"** - every window is listed in the order reported, as a real table with two columns per row; the item is absent when only the week was reported.
- **No roadmap toggle** - the panel carries no "Spend what's left on the roadmap" toggle.
