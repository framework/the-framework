Provides the chip of the launcher's row above its box: a small, bordered, rounded label in muted text, one line high, holding an icon and a few words that say one thing. A plain chip is not a button. The same look is offered on its own, so the button of a menu (the "Run on" pick) can be drawn as a chip too. A chip never grows past the room it is given: its words are cut short, ending in an ellipsis, and its icon stays.
