The one reading of a ticket's `Priority:` key, shared by the ticket list, the ticket detail page and the sort menu so they can never drift into three different interpretations. The scale runs 0 to 10 — 10 means act immediately, 0 means only if there is capacity — and a ticket that names no priority, or names something that is not a number, counts as having none. Colour follows the same scale: 8 and above reads as danger, 5 to 7 as a warning, and everything below that, including "no priority at all", stays muted.
