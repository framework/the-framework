What the tests cover:

- **Reading a priority** - a ticket's priority is read as a whole number on the 0 to 10 scale, zero included.
- **No priority** - a ticket that names no priority, or names something that is not a number, counts as having no priority at all rather than as an unusable value.
- **How urgent it looks** - 8 and above reads in the danger color, 5 to 7 in the warning color, 4 and below in the muted color, and a ticket with no priority reads muted too.
