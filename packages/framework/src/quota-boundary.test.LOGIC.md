What the tests cover:

- **Reading the reset time** - the coding agent's prose becomes the exact instant it names: "Jul 25 at 7am (UTC)", a time with minutes ("7:30pm"), and "12am" and "12pm" as midnight and noon; the comma phrasing ("Jul 25, 7am (UTC)", "Jul 26, 4:50am (Europe/Berlin)") reads the same as the "at" phrasing; the year is the one that puts the reset near now, so "Jan 3" read on New Year's Eve lands in the coming year and "Dec 30" read on January 2 in the year just ended; a named zone is honored with the offset in force on that date, on both sides of a daylight-saving change; prose that cannot be placed ("soon", an unknown month, "13am", a zone name that does not exist) yields no time rather than a guess.
- **The boundary rises continuously** - at the first instant of the week nothing is allowed yet, so no whole seventh is handed out up front; a quarter of the way through the week the boundary is a plain 25 percent and halfway it is 50, not rounded to a day; the day of the week, 1 to 7, steps at the exact second the week's own day rolls over rather than at midnight; the boundary reaches 100 percent exactly as the week resets, so a day before the reset it reads day 7 with six sevenths allowed; a week already over reads as its last day, never as a day eight.
- **Measuring the account's week** - with the reset five days away the boundary is on day 3, a reading of 16 percent used has room, and a reading of 50 percent used is reached and named by the window's own label ("Current week (all models)").
- **The selected model's own week** - working on `claude-fable-5`, the "Current week (Fable)" window binds beside the account's week and is the one reached when it is spent; working on `claude-sonnet-5`, a spent Fable week does not stop the work and only the account's week is in force; with no model given, only the account's week is measured.
- **Nothing when the week cannot be placed** - no windows at all, a week window with no reset prose, and a week window whose reset prose cannot be read ("later") all yield no status.
- **The line is the boundary until the user moves it** - with no offset the offset is 0 and the line sits exactly on the boundary.
- **The spend offset moves the line, not the boundary** - pulled back below what is already spent, the same reading is over the line while the boundary is untouched; pushed forward, an account that had reached the boundary has room again.
- **The line stops at the week's ends** - an offset dragged far below the week pins the line at 0 and one dragged far above pins it at 100, and a reading of 99 percent used is still under a line pinned at the top, so work may still run.
