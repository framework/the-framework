What the tests cover:

- **Reading the reset time** - the coding agent's prose becomes the exact instant it names: "Jul 25 at 7am (UTC)", a time with minutes ("7:30pm"), and "12am" and "12pm" as midnight and noon; the comma phrasing ("Jul 25, 7am (UTC)", "Jul 26, 4:50am (Europe/Berlin)") reads the same as the "at" phrasing; the year is the one that puts the reset near now, so "Jan 3" read on New Year's Eve lands in the coming year and "Dec 30" read on January 2 in the year just ended; a named zone is honored with the offset in force on that date, on both sides of a daylight-saving change; prose that cannot be placed ("soon", an unknown month, "13am", a zone name that does not exist) yields no time rather than a guess.
- **The boundary rises continuously** - at the first instant of the week nothing is allowed yet, so no whole seventh is handed out up front; a quarter of the way through the week the boundary is a plain 25 percent and halfway it is 50, not rounded to a day; the day of the week, 1 to 7, steps at the exact second the week's own day rolls over rather than at midnight; the boundary reaches 100 percent exactly as the week resets, so a day before the reset it reads day 7 with six sevenths allowed; a week already over reads as its last day, never as a day eight.
- **The week's boundary** - with the reset five days away the boundary is on day 3, and it is the one the account's week places, a spent per-model week with an earlier reset beside it changing nothing.
- **Nothing when the week cannot be placed** - no windows at all, a week window with no reset prose, and a week window whose reset prose cannot be read ("later") all yield no boundary.
