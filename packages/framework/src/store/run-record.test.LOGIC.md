What the tests cover:

- **The card** - a full status snapshot round-trips through the `logs` skill's card: the skill's eleven fields come first in the card, in its order (id, start time, status, end time, intent, driver, model, branch, pull request, ticket, cost), everything else sits under `caller`, and reading the card back yields the identical snapshot; even a bare snapshot writes a `caller` carrying its last-updated time; a card written by another program, with no `caller` at all, unfolds with its end time as the last-updated time, or its start time when it has not ended.
- **The diary** - events round-trip through the diary: the coding agent's text becomes a `said` line, a turn's final answer a `result` line keeping its session id and usage, a usage event a `cost` line whether or not it carries a price, and an end an `ended` line at `done`, at `stopped` with its detail, or at `failed` with its detail; the driver's start and action events and the session event are written as they are; reading the diary back yields the original events.
