An answered gate collapsed to one row, so what was decided stays visible without taking more of the page than a line: a green check, the question, and "Expand" or "Collapse" at the end. Expanded, it lists every option of the gate with a check on the picked ones and the rest dimmed, or the line "Accepted none" when the answer picked nothing. It is used by the agent's transcript for a gate that was answered (`EventList.tsx`).
