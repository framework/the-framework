What the tests cover, for the project home [1], with stand-ins for its sections:

- **No documents in the column** - a project that has a `PLAN` document shows the launcher and the open questions, and no "Docs" section, heading or document name; the page does not read the project's documents at all, since they are the right rail's "Docs" tab.
- **Laid out as a chat** - the open questions come before the launcher; they sit in the centred column that scrolls, and the launcher is outside what scrolls.

## Glossary

[1] project home: a project's own page with the launcher (the Start form) and its composer (the prompt editor, also used to say something to an agent).
