Provides the centered modal box the dashboard uses for a small form, such as adding a device: a darkened backdrop, a titled box at most 34rem wide with a "Close" cross in its header, shown and hidden by the host. Unlike the confirm dialog in `confirm-dialog.tsx`, it is not defended: Escape, a click on the backdrop or the cross all close it, since abandoning a half-filled form is not a decision to protect.
