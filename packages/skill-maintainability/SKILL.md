disable-model-invocation: true
---

Refactor a part of the project to make it as maintainable as possible. Nobody will answer you: never ask, decide yourself. The part is what follows the command: a folder, a file, or a feature in words; when nothing follows it, it is the whole project. Look for maintainability red flags in it, and fix them. Commit each change on your branch. Then publish the work: push your branch and open its pull request, and leave the merge to a person. The pull request's body lists each red flag you found and how you fixed it. If you find nothing worth fixing, say so and stop, publishing nothing.
