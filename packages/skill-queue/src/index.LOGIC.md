The package's entry point, re-exporting everything the daemon and the dashboard use: the queue file's name from `names.ts`, the reading, placement and removal rules and the two edits of `queue.ts`, the branch sync and write cycle of `store.ts`, the command of `cli.ts`, and the skill's locations from `bin-dir.ts`. It holds no logic of its own; `names.ts` is also reachable as its own entry point for browser code.
