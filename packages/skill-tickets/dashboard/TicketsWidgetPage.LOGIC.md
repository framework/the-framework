The widget's one page, routed on the segments the dashboard hands it after `/tickets` (the rule of `../src/widget.ts`): none is the list of every project's tickets (`TicketsPage.tsx`, given the projects that have the package); a project then a ticket's filename is that ticket's page (`TicketDetailPage.tsx`) — where the dashboard sends a link to `tickets/<file>` in that project, by its convention; `plan` after those is the ticket's plan (`TicketPlanPage.tsx`). Anything else reads "No such ticket page." under the way back to the list.
