The package's entry point, re-exporting everything the daemon and the dashboard use: the names and gates of `names.ts`, the readers of `tickets.ts`, the claim and release operations of `locks.ts`, the holder rule of `holder.ts`, the branch checkout and sync of `store.ts`, the command of `cli.ts`, and the skill's locations from `bin-dir.ts`. It holds no logic of its own; `names.ts` is also reachable as its own entry point for browser code.
