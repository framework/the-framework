The "Autonomous AI" section — "Focus on what matters, let AI do the rest." — showing what the product does without being asked, split into two cards.

Autonomous product management: testing and reviewing, researching, planning and prioritizing tickets, turning team conversations into tickets, saving those conversations to `conversations/<DATE>_<TOPICS_SLUG>.md`, and market research. Autonomous coding: quick wins, quality refactoring, UX improvements, and consensual work.

Two notes keep the promise honest: agents still ask for confirmation on non-obvious decisions, and the user picks how much autonomy they want.
