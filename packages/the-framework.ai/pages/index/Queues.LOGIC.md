The second building block of "How it works", "2. Queues": two cards. The "AI Queue" 🤖 is described as the queue of upcoming AI tasks, added by humans or by agents (autonomously when there is no uncertainty, after human confirmation otherwise); agents populating it themselves is, the site says, what makes AI autonomous; and technically it is just a `TODO_AGENTS.md` file in the user's Git repositories. The "Human Queue" 🙋 is described as the queue of pending human reviews, populated when agents need the user to review important decisions with subtle pros and cons: "It's your cockpit", the site says, and it keeps humans in control.
