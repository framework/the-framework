The landing page's "On this page" navigation: a bar listing the five sections a visitor can jump to — "Stop babysitting", "Autonomous AI", "How it works", "Features" and "Your framework". It sticks to the top of the window once the visitor scrolls past it, so the table of contents stays reachable through the whole page.

## Business logic — TL;DR

- **The bar follows the reader** - the entry for the section currently being read is highlighted, and the address bar's anchor is kept in step, so a copied or bookmarked link points at the section the visitor was actually looking at.
- **Nothing is highlighted on the closing screen** - once the visitor reaches the final call to action, no section is marked as current.
- **Back to top once stuck** - the product logo fades into the bar only while the bar is stuck to the top of the window, and clicking it scrolls smoothly back to the hero.
