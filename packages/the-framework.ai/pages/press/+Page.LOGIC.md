The press page (`/press`): what journalists and community members need to talk about The Framework — the logo, the name, the banner and where the brand sources live — as four short sections under the heading "Press" and the line "Logos, banners, and naming — everything you need to talk about The Framework." Reaching it is deliberate: no link in the site's navigation or footer points here; the page opens by right-clicking the logo in the top navigation (`../index/TopNav.tsx`) or by typing its address.

## Business logic — TL;DR

- **"Logo"** - the mark is called the "hexknot", six interlocking strands forming a hexagon; it is shown "on dark" and "on light", downloadable as `/assets/logo.svg`, and more variants (palettes, sizes) come from the brand playground at `https://brillout.github.io/brand-the-framework/`.
- **"Name"** - the product is written "The Framework" (capital T, capital F) and the npm package is `framework`.
- **"Banner"** - `/banner.jpg` (1200 by 630 pixels, the Open Graph size) is shown, downloadable, and explained as a screenshot of the `/banner` page (`../banner/+Page.tsx`), with a link to the instructions for regenerating it.
- **"Brand assets"** - the logo sources, color palettes and the generator live in the `brillout/brand-the-framework` repository on GitHub; for anything else the visitor is invited to the Discord server.
