The press kit: everything someone needs to write or talk about The Framework.

It covers four things. The logo — the *hexknot*, six interlocking strands forming a hexagon — is previewed on both a dark and a light background to show it works on either, and offered as a download, alongside a link to the brand playground that generates more variants. The name is spelled "The Framework" with a capital T and a capital F, and the npm package is `framework`. The banner image `banner.jpg` is shown and downloadable, with the note that it is a 1200×630 screenshot of the `/banner` page and a link to the instructions for regenerating it. Finally, the logo sources, color palettes and the generator are pointed at their own repository, with an invitation to ask on Discord for anything else.

## Glossary

- **hexknot** — the product's logo mark: six interlocking strands forming a hexagon.
