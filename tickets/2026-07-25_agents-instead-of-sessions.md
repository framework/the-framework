# "Agents" instead of "Sessions"?

## TLDR

Rename the "session" wording to "agent" throughout the UI: "New session" → "New agent", "Recent sessions" → "Recent agents", "No sessions yet." → "No agents yet.", etc. The maintainer likes it but explicitly invites pushback.

## Why it matters

Naming decision that shapes how the product presents itself — "agents" matches the mental model of autonomous workers better than the technical "sessions". Touches copy across the whole dashboard, so it's worth deciding once before more UI accumulates.
