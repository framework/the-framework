# "Agents" instead of "Sessions"?

## TLDR

Rename "Sessions" to "Agents" across the dashboard: "New session" → "New agent", "Recent sessions" → "Recent agents", "No sessions yet." → "No agents yet.", etc. The maintainer likes it but explicitly invites push-back.

## Why it matters

Naming shapes the product's mental model — "agents" sells autonomous workers, "sessions" sells chat transcripts. Worth deciding before the demo video and landing page freeze the wording. (Counter-consideration from #772: the de-facto standard the dashboard imitates, claude.ai/code, says "session".)
