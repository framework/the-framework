# [Dashboard] Show routine work (i.e. cron jobs)

## TLDR

A dashboard list of routine work (cron jobs), each with a "Run now" button and an auto-run checkbox — "[x] Auto-runs in 2 hours" / "[ ] Auto-run" — with the tooltip "Automatically run this prompt on a regular schedule." Suggested to postpone, at least until after the UX paper cuts.

## Why it matters

Recurring prompts (maintenance sweeps, imports, checks) are invisible today; surfacing them with a manual trigger and an auto-run toggle makes the framework's background activity legible and controllable. Explicitly deprioritized behind UX paper-cut work.
