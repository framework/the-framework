# Show tickets as whole page instead of sidebar

## TLDR

Render the tickets view as a full page instead of a sidebar. (Title-only issue — no description on GitHub.)

## Why it matters

Tickets are becoming a first-class surface (Hot tickets lanes, ticket-driven runs); a sidebar caps how much of a ticket — or how many — can be read and acted on. Fits the ongoing dashboard rework direction (#1139, #772).
