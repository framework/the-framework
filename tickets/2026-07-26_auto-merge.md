# Auto-merge

## TLDR

New setting `[ ] Auto-merge`, default false. Should be set to `true` for some prompts, e.g. the "quick-win work" and "consensual work" routines.

## Why it matters

Auto-merge closes the last human gap in the autonomous loop for low-risk work: routine-generated quick wins shouldn't wait on a human click to land. Complements #1173's always-open-a-PR direction.
