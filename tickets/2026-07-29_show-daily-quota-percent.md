# Idea: also show daily `%` used

## TLDR

The Usage panel shows spend in `$` (well liked). Additionally show quota consumption as (1) how much pro-rata quota time it consumed, and (2) `%` of the pro-rata-daily quota.

## Why it matters

Quota is the resource that actually parks runs (see #1490), and dollar spend alone doesn't tell the user whether today's pace is sustainable against the daily budget. Filed by the maintainer explicitly as autonomous-AI fodder — "if it turns out to be a quick-win" (#1334 loop).
