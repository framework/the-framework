# Avoid term "Spike"

## TLDR

Non-technical users aren't familiar with the term "spike" — avoid it in user-facing wording. Sparing use is fine where context makes it okay, e.g. name the routine task "Plan tickets (aka spike)". The maintainer explicitly wants this ticket dogfooded: let autonomous AI pick it up.

## Why it matters

Wording is product surface: jargon in routine names confuses exactly the non-technical audience TF targets. Doubles as a live test of the autonomous quick-win loop (#1334).
