# Project dropdown

## TLDR

Replace the Project collapsible with a multi-select dropdown of all projects, moved to the very top of the sidebar (right below the "The Framework" logo/text) and visually separated so it's clear the selection filters everything. All pages filter to the selected projects (tickets, overview, recent sessions, ...). Dropdown behavior: selected projects shown on top; an "All" value; checkbox click toggles a project; clicking the row outside the checkbox selects only that project; tooltips clarify usage.

## Why it matters

Project scoping is the top-level filter for the whole dashboard; the current collapsible doesn't communicate that and doesn't support multi-project views.
