# [Dogfooding] Auto-refactor

## TLDR

Let AI do refactors like [#1536](https://github.com/gemstack-land/the-framework/pull/1536) (almost) fully autonomously, so the codebase is maintained and improves on its own whenever quota has spare capacity — leaving the human on high-level flows.

## Why it matters

This is the payoff case for autonomous operation: refactoring is exactly the work that never gets prioritized by hand but degrades the codebase when skipped, and it's a good fit for idle quota. The elements exist already but are, in the issue's words, "all half-assed" — judged possible, but needing real work.
